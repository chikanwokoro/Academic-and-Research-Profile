ONGOING RESEARCH01

Computational Glycan
Structural Analysis

Glycans are structurally diverse molecules whose branching patterns and molecular arrangements contribute to important biological processes, including cellular recognition and molecular interactions.

My current research explores the computational analysis of glycan structures, focusing on their topology, branching patterns and structural features. By representing glycan structures computationally and examining their measurable properties, I aim to investigate patterns of structural variation and develop a quantitative understanding of glycan complexity.

The broader research interest is to understand how glycan structural diversity may contribute to biological recognition and function, providing a foundation for further computational investigations in glycobiology.

Research focus
  • Glycan structural representation and analysis
  • Molecular topology and branching patterns
  • Quantitative characterization of structural diversity
  • Computational approaches to glycobiology

Status: Ongoing research
