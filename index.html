02 / RESEARCH

Selected research work

ONGOING PROJECT01

GlycoGraph-ML

Graph-based computational analysis of N-glycan structures.

An ongoing project exploring graph representations and quantitative structural characterisation of N-glycans. The current exploratory analysis considers graph properties such as nodes, edges, depth, branching and terminal residues.

PUBLISHED WORK02

Computational modelling of β-lactamase interactions

Research involving molecular docking and enzyme-kinetics modelling to investigate drug–enzyme interactions. The project is documented through its published paper; original computational files are not currently included in this portfolio.

PUBLISHED WORK03

Allium cepa network pharmacology

Research on bioactive compounds from Allium cepa and downstream protein effectors in diabetes. My contribution included ADMET analysis of seven phytochemicals.
